### Description ###
Torricelli's tank uses a tall water-filled cylinder with three holes at different heights (top, middle, and bottom) to show how water pressure turns into speed.

### Equipment ###
* Long acrylic tube with 3 holes
* Water
* Food colouring
* container

### Instruction ###
Tape the three holes and fill the tube with water. Place a receiving container at its base. Open all three holes and observe the change in the water flow.

### Physics ###
Deeper holes have more water above them, creating higher pressure that shoots water out faster. How far a stream lands depends on both exit speed and fall height. The bottom hole shoots the fastest, the top hole shoots the slowest, and the middle hole lands the farthest because it balances speed and fall time.

### Concepts ###
* Torricelli's law: the speed at which an ideal fluid exits a small hole in a tank is equal to the speed an object gains in free fall from the fluid's surface height above the hole
