### Description ###
A series of pulleys demonstrate the ability to use an equal amount of force to lift several different masses or that the amount of force needed to lift a mass is reduced as more pulleys are used.


### Equipment ###
* system of pulleys
* masses
* force scales
* diagrams of pulley arrangements

### Instruction ###
Arrange the pulleys according to the diagrams. The first setup shows that through the use of pulleys, the same amount of force can lift a different amount of mass. The second setup shows that the amount of force needed to lift a mass is reduced as more pulleys are used.

{% picture IMG_0664.JPG %}

### Physics ###
In an ideal, frictionless pulley system, the tension (T) remains uniform throughout a single continuous rope. The load mass is supported by the vertical strands of rope directly pulling up on the pulley. If a system has 4 supporting strands, the upward force is 4T. Therefore, to balance a load, the required input force is 4 times less yielding a mechanical advantage.

### Concepts ###
Tension: The pulling force transmitted axially through a string, rope, or cable, which stays constant across a single ideal strand.
