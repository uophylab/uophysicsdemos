### Description ###
Surface tension pulls the surface of liquid up. The bigger the ratio between the circumference and the area of the surface, the bigger the effect

### Equipment ###
* Capillary tubes
* Liquid (water, alcohol, etc.)
* Food colouring (Optional)

  
### Instruction ###
Add coloured liquid to the five capillary tubes and compare the different height relative to the diameter of the tubes.
