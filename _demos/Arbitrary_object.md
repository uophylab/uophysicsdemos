### Description ###
Using a Bob, three lines are drawn down from different edges of this arbitrary object in order to determine the centre of mass.


### Equipment ###
* non-uniform flat object
* Bob on a string

### Instruction ###
The object has previously been hung by each of the three nails around its edge. In each case, a mass on a string has been attached to the nail and a line was drawn along the path of the string. The point at which the three lines inter-connect is the centre of mass of the non-uniform flat object.

### Physics ###
When the object hangs from a pivot, gravity pulls downward on its centre of mass. If the centre of mass is not directly below the pivot, this gravitational force creates a lever arm, generating a rotational force called torque. The object will only stop moving when the net torque becomes zero. This zero-torque state occurs exclusively when the centre of mass aligns perfectly on the vertical axis straight beneath the pivot.
