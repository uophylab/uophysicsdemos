### Description ###
Three lines are drawn down from different edges of a non-uniform flat object in order to determine the center of mass.


### Equipment ###
* non-uniform flat object

### Instruction ###
The object has previously been hung by each of the three nails around its edge. In each case, a mass on a string has been attached to the nail and a line was drawn along the path of the string. The point at which the three lines inter-connect is the center of mass of the non-uniform flat object.
