### Description ###
A jet of air is blown across one end of a tube filled with crispy rice or puffed wheat.

### Equipment ###
* Air blower
* Large beaker
* Transparent tube
* Cripsy rice or Puffed wheat

### Instruction ###
Pour the crispy rice inside a large beaker. Place the transparent tube in the crispy rice and set the end of the air flow to the top edge of the gap letting a stream of air straight down the pipe. Watch as the two puffed wheat are propelled out of the beaker!

### Physics ###
Increasing the speed of a fluid (air) decreases its internal static pressure. The fast-moving air between the sheets has lower pressure than the still air outside them. The higher atmospheric pressure on the outer sides pushes the two sheets inward.

### Concepts ###
* Bernoulli's principle: an increase in the speed of a fluid (liquid or gas) occurs simultaneously with a decrease in static pressure or potential energy
