### Description ###
The center of mass of irregular objects allows these objects to balance themselves in precarious positions.


### Equipment ###
* utensils
* glass
* eagle
* cone

### Instruction ###
Balance the utensils arrangement on the edge of the glass. Balance the eagle's beak on the tip of the inverted cone. (see images and movies)
