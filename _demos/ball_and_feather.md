### Description ###
A feather falls more slowly than a ball down a tube due to air resistance. When the tubes are evacuated, both the feather and ball fall with equal speed.

### Equipment ###
* Double tube assembly
* Vacuum pump
* Clear flexible tubing
* Power cable
* Extension cord


### Instruction ###
With both cylinders in air, rotate the tubes by 180 degrees and see what falls first. Repeat the same procedure under vacuum. Does the observation change?
