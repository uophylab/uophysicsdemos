### Description ###
The Magdeburg hemispheres are a pair connected of hemispheres demonstrating the power of atmospheric pressure. When the space between the two hemispheres has lower pressure then the surrounding they become very difficult to separate.

### Equipment ###
* Magdeburg hemispheres
* Vacuum pump
  
### Instruction ###
Apply a few drops of water to one of the surface. Brings the two surfaces together and they should require some force to separate them. (However, they will still slide without friction)
