### Description ###
The maximal height of a liquid in connected vases will be equal.

### Equipment ###
* Coloured water
* Pascal's tubes

### Instruction ###
Fill each tubes with coloured water and observe the water level remains the same no matter the shape of the tube.

### Physics ###
The pressure at any point down inside a stagnant fluid depends solely on the vertical depth beneath the surface, the density of the liquid, and gravitational acceleration, following the equation \(P = \rho gh\). It does not depend on the total volume or shape of the container.

### Concepts ###
* Hydrostatic Pressure (\(P = \rho gh\)): The pressure exerted by a fluid at rest due to the force of gravity, which scales linearly with vertical depth.
