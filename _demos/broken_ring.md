### Description ###
Upon exit, a ball goes off on a tangent as it is rolled around a circular ring with a gap.

### Equipment ###
- Broken ring
- Metal Ball

### Instruction ###
Lightly roll the ball along the edge on the inside of the ring. Catch the ball slightly after it exits the ring.
