### Description ###
A hydraulic press is used to break a piece of wood.

### Equipment ###
* Hydraulic press
* Small piece of wood

### Instruction ###
1. Place the small piece of wood flat and centered on the lower anvil of the hydraulic press.
2. Seal the hydraulic valve and pump the manual lever handle to drive the hydraulic fluid forward.
3. Watch as the pressure steadily builds up immense force until the wood violently splinters, cracks, or snaps under the pressure.

### Physics ###
The core mechanic relies on the law stating that pressure applied to an enclosed fluid is transmitted undiminished throughout the entire liquid body in all directions. Because the fluid is confined, the pressure at the input cylinder is identically equal to the pressure at the output cylinder. Pressure is defined as force divided by area, by making the surface area of the output piston significantly larger than the input piston, a small human input force is multiplied into a massive output force capable of crushing solid targets.

### Concepts ###
* Pascal's Principle: The foundational fluid mechanics rule stating that a pressure change at any point in a confined incompressible fluid is transmitted equally throughout the entire fluid.
* Force Multiplication: The mechanical process of leveraging geometric area ratios within a closed fluid system to magnify an input pushing force.
