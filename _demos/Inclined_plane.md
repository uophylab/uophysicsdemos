### Description ###
A ball rolls down an inclined plane. The angle of the incline is adjustable.

### Equipment ###
* inclined plane
* rod setup
* tennis ball

### Instruction ###
Adjust the inclined plane to the desired angle. Let the ball roll down the plane.
