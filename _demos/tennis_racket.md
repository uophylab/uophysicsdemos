### Description ###
The centre of mass in a tennis racket is at the base of the loop. This is shown when the tennis racket is spun in the air.

### Equipment ###
- Tennis racket

### Instruction ###
Throw the tennis racket in the air such that it rotates rapidly, end over end. The centre of mass of the racket will appear to fly in a parabolic pattern.
