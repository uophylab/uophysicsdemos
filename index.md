Welcome to the uOttawa Physics Demonstrations Repository. This platform is a guide designed for Professors, instructors, TAs, and students looking to bring abstract concepts to life through demos or experiments.
Every demonstration catalogued here is broken down into clear categories with individual demos label as cards. Each demos contain the equipment needed, the setup or the apparatus, the instructions, and the underlying Physics and Key Concepts at play. Explore the collection below to find classroom-tested demos, visualize physical principles, and master the mechanics of the universe.

### Browsing demonstrations
Lecture physics demonstrations available at the university of Ottawa can be found on this site.  They are sorted following the [http://physicslearning.colorado.edu/bib/bibMain.asp PIRA Demonstration Classification Scheme]. You can also use our search engine located at the top of this page.

### Borrowing a demonstration for a lecture
If you need to borrow a demonstration for your class, please contact the Undergraduate Physics laboratory staff in person at STM 377, by phone at x6766, or [mailto:phylab@uottawa.ca by email] at least 48 hours before your lecture.
